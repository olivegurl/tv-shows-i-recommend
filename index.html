HistoricalFiction

  1. The Crown: This is a great tv show for people who are interested in the royal family. There are 6 seasons, and although the sixth season was the most weak in my opinion, I still think it is a good show for lovers of Downtown Abbey . It follows Queen Elizabeth II as she ascends to the throne after her father's death. It covers many well known events in British history, but also some more obscure ones. Overall, I would recommend this for those whose favorite genre is historical fiction.

  2. My Lady Jane: A tv show that was recently cancelled, it follows a woman named Jane who is being forced to marry a man she doesn't know, Lord Dudley. Sounds like every other historical fiction tv show, right? Well, she also has to marry him to save her family from destitution, she finds out he's hot, and oh, there are magic beings called Ethians who have been hiding in plain sight? A laugh-out-loud romp that was gone too soon. #SaveMyLadyJane

  3. Anne with An E: Are we starting to see a trend with the tv shows I like (cough canceled cough). Well, this one is great, despite not having an ending (but if you're thinking that, go read the books!). The show follows titular character Anne Shirley as she gets adopted by brother and sister Matthew & Marilla Cuthbert, going to live with them in Avonlea. But oops, they wanted a boy, and Anne is a girl! What follows is a pretty loyal adaptation of the beloved book series, except showrunner Moira Walley-Beckett adds her own flares, which I believe make the show that much better. Without spoilers, there's the exploration of more of Anne's personality and background as an orphan, completely new characters, and so much more. These additions make Avonlea (and PEI and Canada) seem that much more historically accurate. Love love love and will never get over it.

ScienceFiction

  1. Black Mirror: Ultimately one of my absolute favorite shows, this series is an anthology. I had to put that in italics because it's important to know. An anthology means that none of the episodes are connected. So each has its own story. Therefore, if you're going to watch this exploration-of-technology-and-dystopia, please for the love of God, WATCH WHATEVER EPISODE YOU WANT. DO NOT START WITH THE FIRST EPISODE. It is very disturbing and (I think) was made to psych people out of watching the rest of it? Anyways. Do what I did and start with Season 2 episode 2, White Bear. It's perfect. Or start with any other episode that sounds intriguing to you. Save S1E1 as second last, and bleach your eyes with one of the lighter episodes, like San Junipero. Ok. PSA over.

  2. In the Flesh: A bit of a deep cut for you here with this one. The cancelled show (see what I mean? I'm a masochist or something) follows a Kieran, a young man who has recently been released from the holding facility where he was being treated for being a Partially Deceased Syndrome Sufferer. AKA a zombie. Yes, you read that right. Treated . Britain found a cure and started administering it to PDSS', sending them home with their meds and some foundation and contact lenses so that they look like their old self. Except Kieran has to go back to his small village where a)his sister was part of the zombie army and b)no one really knows that he died?? A great 2 season show with an excellent (and sad) soundtrack by Keaton Henson. Amazing representation for something that was released in 2013. Not sure where you can watch it lol, I have it on DVD.

Fantasy

  1. Merlin: Ok, finally a show that wasn't canceled. It follows Merlin, a young wizard who is being sent to train under an older wizard in the kingdom of Avalon. Except he didn't realize he'd be helping Arthur, King Uther's son. Nor did he realize hiding his magic would be so difficult. Confession: I haven't actually finished this series, but I wanted to add it to the list because I love it. Ok, sometimes it's a bit cheesy and I ask myself why I am bingeing a children' show, but it's so good! I'm excited to see where it goes (even if people online say the ending was very disappointing).

  2. Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power: Uh oh, another unpopular selection. But I like it! Although, if I have to explain it, I'll need a bit to figure it out. Umm. Because I only saw the movies for the rest of this series. Soooo. I guess it's about how the Elves are trying to make some rings while grappling with the rise of Sauron, but the elves don't believe the one elf's (Galadriel) claim that he's back. And then we get to meet some humans and another elf and some dwarves and some Harfoots. I think that's the best I can do for a description. Please see a better one here. I went in blind and I'm loving it so far. I haven't finished s2 yet so no spoilers please.

  3. House of the Dragon: Another one that I am having trouble explaining. I guess it's about the Targaryen family and their quest to find the rightful heir to the throne? Add in the usual GOT family dynamics and some dragons and some war and I guess it becomes a whole...mess. I really liked it. I went in blind, having never seen the original GOT. And in some ways I'm glad because apparently this one is a lot tamer than the original. So yeah. Love it. Love the dragons. #TeamBlack
