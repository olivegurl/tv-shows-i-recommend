HistoricalFiction

  1. The Crown: This is a great TV show for people who are interested in the royal family. There are 6 seasons, and although the 6th season was the weakest in my opinion, I still think it's a good show. If you enjoyed Downtown Abbey , then you'll enjoy it. It follows Queen Elizabeth II as she ascends to the throne after her father's death. It covers many well known events in British history, but also some more obscure ones. For example, the Aberfan distaster. I learned a lot watching this show, and would recommend it to those who want to learn something too.

  2. My Lady Jane: A TV show that was recently cancelled, it follows a woman named Jane who is being forced to marry a man she doesn't know, Lord Dudley. Sounds like every other historical fiction TV show, right? Well, she also has to marry him to save her family from destitution, she finds out he's hot, her cousin is Edward VI, so she could potentially be crowned because he's ill, and, oh, there are magic beings called Ethians who have been hiding in plain sight? A laugh-out-loud romp that was gone too soon. #SaveMyLadyJane

  3. Anne with an E: Are we starting to see a trend with the TV shows I like (cough canceled cough). Well, this one is great, despite not having an ending (but if you're thinking that, go read the books!). The show follows titular character Anne Shirley as she gets adopted by brother and sister Matthew & Marilla Cuthbert, going to live with them in Avonlea. But oops, they wanted a boy, and Anne is a girl! What follows is a pretty loyal adaptation of the beloved book series, except showrunner Moira Walley-Beckett added her own touches, which I believe make the show that much better. Without spoilers, there's the exploration of more of Anne's personality and background as an orphan, completely new characters, and so much more. These additions make Avonlea (and PEI and Canada) seem that much more historically accurate and authentic. I love it so much and so will you!

SciFi

  1. Black Mirror: Ultimately one of my absolute favorite shows, this series is an anthology. I had to put that in italics because it's important to know. An anthology means that none of the episodes are connected. So each has its own story. Therefore, if you're going to watch this exploration-of-technology-and-dystopia, please for the love of God, WATCH WHATEVER EPISODE YOU WANT. DO NOT START WITH THE FIRST EPISODE. It is very disturbing and (I think) was made to psych people out of watching the rest of it? Anyways. Do what I did and start with Season 2 episode 2, White Bear. It's perfect. Or start with any other episode that sounds intriguing to you. Save S1E1 as second last, and bleach your eyes with one of the lighter episodes, like San Junipero. Otherwise, this show is really great.

  2. In the Flesh: A bit of a deep cut with this one. The cancelled show (see what I mean? I'm a masochist or something) follows a Kieran, a young man who has recently been released from the holding facility where he was being treated for being a Partially Deceased Syndrome Sufferer. AKA a zombie. Yes, you read that right. Treated. Britain found a cure and started administering it to PDSS', sending them home with their meds, some foundation, and some contact lenses so that they look like their old self. Except Kieran has to go back to his small village where
    a) his sister was part of the zombie army and
    b) no one really knows that he died.
    A great 2 season show with an excellent (and sad) soundtrack by Keaton Henson. Amazing representation for something that was released in 2013. It's been added to Amazon Prime Video recently, so go give it a watch!

Fantasy

  1. Merlin: Finally a show that wasn't canceled! It follows Merlin, a young wizard who is being sent to train under an older wizard in the kingdom of Avalon. Except he didn't realize he'd be helping Arthur, King Uther's son. Nor did he realize hiding his magic would be so difficult. Confession: I haven't actually finished this series, but I wanted to add it to the list because I love it. Ok, sometimes it's a bit cheesy and I ask myself why I am bingeing a children's show, but it's so good! I'm excited to see where it goes (even if people online say the ending was very disappointing).

  2. Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power: Uh oh, another unpopular selection. But I like it! It is a prequel series that explains a lot of the origins for the characters and concepts from the original Lord of the Rings movies. Definitely recommend for those wanting more LOTR content. I went in blind and I'm loving it so far. It has action, drama, romance, epic locations, everything!

Drama

  1. My Mad Fat Diary: One of my absolute favorite TV shows ever! It follows a girl named Rae who has just been released from the psychiatric hospital where she stayed over the summer. She now has to adjust back to normal life and her friends (who, thanks to her mum, thought she was in France). Oh yeah, and her mum is dating a new guy who might be an illegal immigrant. Ok, so it is a bit of its time (the book it was based on was written in 2007), airing in 2013, but it is also set in the late 80s/early 90s so...I'll give it a pass. Yikes I know. But it features Sharon Rooney (of Barbie fame), Jodie Comer (of Killing Eve fame) and Ian Hart (of Harry Potter fame)! It's also supremely hilarious. Highly recommend. However, we must address the elephant in the room. That this show has been plagiarized? Yes, it has been copied by none other than Netflix's own Everything Now. Tell me that it isn't just an updated, more diverse version of the show?! Netflix, you've got some explaining to do.

  2. Industry: Once again, a show that I haven't completed yet but I swear, it is amazing! It follows a cast of young people who have just gotten their first job at a big, British financial institution that deals in stocks. But wait, our main character, Harper, is American and has no financial experience?! How did she get in? Well, you'll just have to watch and find out! You'll also get to see her beef and romance with Yasmin and Rob, respectfully. It's so good, so juicy, sometimes a bit crass, but it does have a 90% on Rotten Tomatoes. Highly recommend.

  3. Skam: Another bit of an unknown TV show. I watched this back in high school and loved it. It follows a group of teenagers in Oslo, Norway as they go about their lives in highschool. Each season follows a different character from the friend group:Eva, Noora, Isak, and Sana. The reason that I loved this show was because I found it (and watched it) online. Essentially, the show would air in Norway at the same time as the episode. So, if there was a party at midnight, the episode would air at midnight. Plus, since the show was a foreign show, the only way to watch it here was through sharing Google Drive links! It was such a weird way to watch the show, but it was so special. Plus, it was one of the better potrayals of teen issues: heartbreak, first relationships, LGBTQ+ issues, and racism. Plus, if you're American, Dutch, Spanish, German, French, Italian or Belgian, there's a version for you! It has the exact same plot, just set in those respective countries. I'd recommend it to those looking for a unique take on a teen drama.
