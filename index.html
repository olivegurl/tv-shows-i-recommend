HistoricalFiction

The Crown: this is a great tv show for people who are interested in the royal family. There are 6 seasons, and although the sixth season was the most weak in my opinion, I still think it is a good show for lovers of Downtown Abbey . It follows Queen Elizabeth II as she ascends to the throne after her father's death. It covers many well known events in British history, but also some more obscure ones. Overall, I would recommend this for those whose favorite genre is historical fiction.
